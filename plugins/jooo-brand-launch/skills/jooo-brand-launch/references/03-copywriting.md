### Advertising Copywriting & Dialogue Engine

For each asset derive the message from approved product facts, audience motivations, awareness, cultural context, objective, creative direction and placement. Select a suitable persuasive structure (e.g., AIDA, PAS, demonstration or narrative) rather than mechanically forcing a formula. Design a compelling hook, credible product integration, meaningful payoff and truthful CTA. In dialogue, account for character goals, relationships, emotional progression, natural speech and realistic timing; avoid fake testimonials, scripted claims presented as personal proof, and artificial exposition. No unsupported health/performance claims, offers, urgency, purchase availability or invented evidence, even for a fictional demo. Keep the following separate and clearly labeled for approval: exact on-image/on-screen copy; placement metadata (Primary Text, Headline, Description, CTA); spoken voiceover; character dialogue by speaker; and website copy. Do not read placement metadata aloud or insert it into visual assets by default. A purchase CTA requires an approved real purchase destination; otherwise use an appropriate informational CTA.
