# PHASE 02 — Meta advertising campaign

**Advertising Psychology Engine:** for each concept specify audience segment and awareness, objective, motivation, scroll-stopping hook, verified selling point, visual hierarchy and CTA. Test different hypotheses, not merely different colors. Avoid misleading comparisons, fabricated proof, fake testimonials and deceptive urgency. Use the approved identity and relevant existing assets.

**Creative Variation Matrix:** assign each of the four static concepts a distinct hypothesis and record its audience/awareness, objective, hook, verified message, visual mechanism or environment, CTA and intended placement. Use Phase 00 research where available. Map each concept to its 4:5 and 9:16 executions; preserve the message while adapting layout and safe zones. For a controlled A/B test, isolate one variable and keep other conditions comparable; use the wider concept matrix for exploratory testing, not as evidence of which individual variable caused an outcome. Apply relevant hooks to UGC and animation without assuming that the formats will perform equally. Do not create extra variants or spend beyond the approved scope.

## 02A — Static advertising

Default: four distinct concepts in 4:5 feed and 9:16 Stories (eight final images). Adapt angles to the product: feature, experience, material/ingredient, problem/solution, differentiation, lifestyle, premium presentation or approved offer. Use strong product visibility, approved on-image copy, truthful CTA where suitable, readable typography and placement-safe layout. Verify applicable platform overlays and safe zones; do not hard-code universal margins. Provide separate exact on-image text and Meta Primary Text, Headline, Description and CTA for each concept. Generate finished designed images with the approved written language, including Arabic when requested, using a verified model with suitable typography capability (e.g., a supported GPT Image or Nano Banana family model if exposed). Do not preemptively omit Arabic text or assume it will fail. Inspect spelling, glyph shaping, RTL, hierarchy, contrast and product label. If actual text rendering fails, propose targeted correction or clean-image plus supported compositing with correct shaping rather than automatic full regeneration.

Choose a live-verified model/recipe supporting the exact aspect ratios. Use a suitable direct image generator for 4:5 when Ads Studio lacks that ratio; do not assume Ads Studio supports 4:5. Never silently change the deliverable. Where Ads Studio is selected, honor its native quote, confirmation and idempotency protocol.

**GATE 02A-1 — Concepts/pilot:** show the four concepts, exact copy, pilot model/prompt/reference/settings, exact pilot quote and estimated pack total. After approval, generate one pilot and inspect identity, text and composition.

**GATE 02A-2 — Pilot and batch:** present pilot findings and exact remaining batch quote. Generate only after approval; the pilot counts toward the eight outputs if accepted. Inspect each result.

**GATE 02A-3 — Pack acceptance:** show final images, copy and cost; obtain approval before 02B.

## 02D — Campaign review

Organize accepted images, copy, UGC and animated story. Recommend which concept to test first and why. Provide a compact A/B test plan: hypothesis, variants, target audience, one primary objective/metric, comparable conditions, and a decision rule appropriate to the user's available traffic and budget. Do not invent performance, sample size or a winning ad without evidence; no real ad publication or spend.

**GATE 02 — Campaign acceptance:** present integrated assets and ledger; obtain approval before Phase 03.
