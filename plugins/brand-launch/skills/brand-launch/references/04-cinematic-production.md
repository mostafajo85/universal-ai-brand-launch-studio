# PHASE 01 — Cinematic product commercial

Default: two finished 30-second ads, landscape 16:9 first and then a separately composed 9:16 adaptation; Seedance 2.5 visuals, ElevenLabs voiceover and one Shared Audio Master. Use the approved duration and suitable supported resolution within budget; 30 seconds is the default unless the user approves another runtime. If a continuous generation cannot meet the approved specification, plan and quote a multi-clip edit. A cinematic commercial may serve as a website asset when suitable, but a scroll-controlled film may require dedicated footage.

**Cinematic Intelligence Engine:** create an original product-specific visual narrative with a compelling opening, progressive development, meaningful signature moment, product reveal, clear hero/brand recognition and a truthful closing message. Derive timing from the actual approved recording; do not force fixed beat lengths. Design shot scale, lens character, camera movement, spatial blocking, choreography, depth, composition, lighting, material response, physical behavior, color, pacing and synchronized sound. Favor coherent visual storytelling over gratuitous effects. Use actual bound product media. Follow the shared copywriting, audio and post-production engines in the shared engines in references 03, 07 and 08.

**GATE 01A — Concept and voiceover:** present treatment, proposed timed structure, exact voiceover script, voice, tags, audio plan, scope and speech quote. After approval, generate the narration, measure its duration and prepare the landscape shot plan around the actual recording. Do not generate video before the voiceover is accepted.

**GATE 01A-V — Landscape production:** show actual narration, audio QC, final timed treatment, optimized video prompt(s), reference binding, model/settings, planned edit/mix and full remaining quote. Obtain explicit audio acceptance and authorization for the specified generation and post-production. Generate, assemble and mix the landscape commercial; retrieve and inspect actual evidence. Preserve the approved shared audio master and available stems.

**GATE 01B — Landscape acceptance and vertical production:** present the finished landscape video, QC and ledger. If accepted, propose a separately framed 9:16 adaptation with platform-safe composition, the same narration file, story timing and shared audio mix, optimized prompt(s), references and quote. Obtain approval before generating/assembling the vertical version. Do not merely crop unless expressly approved. If rejected, follow targeted recovery rather than advancing.

**GATE 01C — Commercial package acceptance:** present both completed versions, settings, asset IDs, shared audio identity, QC and costs. The user may explicitly defer the vertical version. Obtain acceptance before Phase 02; accepted assets remain standalone deliverables.
